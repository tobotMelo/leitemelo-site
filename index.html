Questions

What materials can you work with?

Wood, metal, leather, acrylic, glass, slate, ceramic and more. Ask us about anything else.

Can I supply my own item?

Often yes. Tell us what it is and we'll confirm it's suitable before starting.

Do you take small orders?

Yes, from single personalized gifts to batches for businesses and events.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted individually based on item, size, design and quantity. Message us or request a free quote.

What file types do you accept?

Vector files (SVG, AI, PDF, DXF) work best; high-resolution PNG/JPG photos are fine too.

Do you ship?

Local pickup and shipping are both available. Ask for details when you order.
